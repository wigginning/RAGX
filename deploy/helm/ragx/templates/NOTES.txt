== Quick smoke (kind / kubectl) ==
  # build & load images first (kind load docker-image ragx:full), then:
  helm install ragx ./deploy/helm/ragx -n ragx --create-namespace
  kubectl -n ragx wait --for=condition=Ready pod -l app.kubernetes.io/instance=ragx --timeout=300s
  kubectl -n ragx port-forward svc/ragx 18000:8000 &
  curl -fsS http://localhost:18000/v1/health
